3. Crops, livestock and remedies

Crops mentioned

Rice and cucurbits dominate; chilli is the top single vegetable.

Products recommended in answers

Malathion, rogor and bavistin are the go‑to advice. Urea, SSP and MOP dominate nutrient answers. Standard replies could be templated.

Livestock and fisheries

5. Recommended actions

  • Prioritise advisory content for the top crop‑problem pairs: rice stem borer, potato late blight, cucurbit fruit fly, brinjal borer, chilli leaf curl, wilt in tomato and brinjal.
  • Add a triage question (crop, symptom, stage) for vague calls like “agriculture related problem”.
  • Route PM‑Kisan and KCC queries to a scheme FAQ: 8% of calls, highly repetitive.
  • Re‑run on the full 177k rows, using original record counts, before quoting any percentages.
Caveats: every sampled record_count is 1, and the raw data has ~90,821 duplicate query texts, so call-volume ranking is lost. Some hierarchy labels are mismatched (for example the parent “Fisheries” is mostly expert transfers). Human validation is still pending.
